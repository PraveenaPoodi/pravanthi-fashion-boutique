PRAVANTHI FASHION BOUTIQUE

A responsive, multi-page boutique website.

OPENING THE WEBSITE
1. Extract this ZIP folder.
2. Open the Pravanthi-Fashion-Boutique folder.
3. Double-click index.html, or open the folder in Visual Studio Code.

PAGES
Home, About Us, Collections, Baby Dresses, Kids’ Frocks, Girls’ Party Wear, Traditional Dresses, Wedding & Bridal Wear, Offers, Custom Dress Studio, Customer Experiences, Contact Us, Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

CONTACT SETUP
The boutique's real WhatsApp number was not provided. To activate direct WhatsApp order and enquiry messages, open config.js and enter the country code and phone number as digits in whatsappNumber. Until then, the website directs customers to the Instagram profile linked in the footer. No invented phone number or email address is used.

PRODUCT PHOTOGRAPHS
The supplied fashion images are AI-generated visual references, not verified photographs of current stock. Each product currently has one available photograph. The image opens in a larger accessible viewer. Additional angles are not fabricated or duplicated. Confirm the actual garment, colour, fabric, price and availability with the boutique before ordering.

ACCESSIBILITY
The pages include skip links, semantic landmarks, labelled controls, keyboard-operable navigation and product image viewing, visible focus styles, responsive layouts, text-size controls, high-contrast mode and live status messages. Please test the finished site with NVDA and keyboard navigation before publishing.

PRICES AND POLICIES
Displayed product prices and offers are editable website catalogue content and must be confirmed against actual stock before taking orders. The privacy and terms pages provide clear customer-facing website policies; have them reviewed for your actual business practices and applicable law before launch.

Footer contact details: praveenapoodi@gmail.com and one Instagram link.
