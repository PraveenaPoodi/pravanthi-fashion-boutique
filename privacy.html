Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Pravanthi Fashion Boutique respects your privacy. This page explains how information may be used when you browse this website or contact the boutique.

Information you choose to share

If you contact the boutique, you may choose to share your name, email address, phone number, delivery details, measurements, order preferences or message content. Please share only the details needed to answer your enquiry or process an order.

How information is used

  • To respond to product, size, custom-design and order enquiries.
  • To confirm product details, availability, delivery arrangements and order updates.
  • To provide customer support and improve the browsing experience.

Third-party services

When you choose to open Instagram, you will use that service under its own privacy terms. This website does not control how those platforms collect or process information.

Data storage and security

Please do not send payment-card details, passwords or other highly sensitive information through enquiry messages. Any order information shared directly with the boutique should be handled carefully and accessed only when needed for customer service.

Your choices

You may choose what information to share and may contact the boutique to ask about information you have provided. Use the Instagram contact link on this website to reach the boutique.

Children’s information

This website presents clothing for children, but enquiries and orders should be made by a parent or legal guardian. Do not submit a child’s personal information unless it is necessary for an order.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when website features or business practices change. The latest version will appear on this page.

This policy describes the website’s intended enquiry practices and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified legal professional.
