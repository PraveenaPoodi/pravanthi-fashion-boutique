About Pravanthi Fashion Boutique

From Little Princesses to Beautiful Brides.

Welcome to Pravanthi Fashion Boutique, a place to explore clothing ideas for little ones, growing girls and women. The boutique brings together baby dresses, kids’ frocks, girls’ party wear, traditional outfits and wedding and bridal styles in one collection.

Our collection is designed around the moments people love to dress up for: welcoming a baby, birthdays, family gatherings, festivals, celebrations and weddings. From soft-looking baby styles to festive frocks and elegant occasion wear, there are choices for different ages and occasions.

Our collections

  • Baby dresses: gentle-looking styles for babies aged 1–6 months and 7–12 months.
  • Kids’ frocks: playful frocks and comfortable-looking styles for growing children.
  • Girls’ party wear: colourful outfits for birthdays and special celebrations.
  • Traditional dresses: festive and traditional-inspired outfits for family occasions.
  • Wedding and bridal wear: special-occasion styles for wedding celebrations.

Custom dress enquiries

Have a particular colour, occasion or design in mind? You can send a custom outfit enquiry with the outfit type, age or size, preferred colour and design details. The boutique can then discuss whether the requested style is possible, along with fabric, price and expected timing.

Our promise

We aim to make it easier to explore styles and ask questions. Before confirming an order, please check the actual garment, fabric, colour, measurements, final price, availability and delivery details directly with the boutique. Images on this website are visual style references and may not represent an item currently in stock.
