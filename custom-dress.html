Custom Dress Studio

A look shaped around your occasion, style and preferences.

Have a design in mind? Tell us about the outfit you would love to wear or gift. We welcome enquiries for baby dresses, kids’ frocks, girls’ party wear, traditional outfits, festive looks, saree-to-dress transformations and bridal wear.

Saree-to-Dress Transformation

Give a favourite saree a fresh look by transforming it into a modern dress. Share your saree photo and preferred dress style with the boutique to discuss possible designs.

To enquire: Select “Saree-to-dress transformation” in the custom enquiry form and describe your preferred style.

How a custom enquiry works

  1. Share the occasion, preferred outfit type and inspiration.
  2. Tell us your preferred colours, approximate size or measurements and any design details.
  3. The boutique will discuss fabric and design feasibility, price and expected timeline.
  4. Once the details are agreed, you can decide whether to proceed with the order.

Submitting this form prepares your message and opens the boutique Instagram profile. Paste the copied message into an Instagram direct message. You can also reach the boutique through Instagram.
